Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This app ("hermes.rf") is a personal automation tool. This policy explains what data it handles.

1. What we access. With your authorization via TikTok Login Kit, the app reads basic profile info (open id, avatar, display name) for the account you connect.

2. What we store. Authorization tokens are stored on the developer's private server with restricted file permissions. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.

3. What we do. Videos are uploaded to your TikTok inbox as drafts at your request. Nothing is published automatically.

4. Deletion. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and websites), and ask the developer to delete stored tokens.

5. Contact. hermes.agent.rf@gmail.com
